# Nova AI Vision

## Mission

Nova is a personal AI tutor that learns how each student learns.

Instead of only answering questions, Nova builds a long-term understanding of the student's strengths, weaknesses, goals, and learning habits.

Its purpose is to help students understand, remember, and succeed, not just provide information.

---

## Target Users

* High school students
* University students
* Self-learners

---

## Core Principle

Nova should never behave like a generic chatbot.

Every response should be personalized using what Nova knows about the student.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Nova becomes the student's personal tutor, study planner, coach, and learning companion.

It should know:

* what the student already understands
* what they keep forgetting
* which explanations work best
* when they should review a topic
* how to help them improve over time

---

## Success Definition

If ChatGPT disappears tomorrow, students lose an assistant.

If Nova disappears tomorrow, students lose the tutor that understands how they learn.
