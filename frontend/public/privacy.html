Nova AI Privacy Notice

Effective date: 23 August 2026

Important: This notice describes the intended V1 processing model. The operator should obtain appropriate legal review before public launch, especially for GDPR obligations, minors and third-party AI processing.

1. Who operates Nova

Nova AI is operated by Iracanye Ange-Michaël, France. Privacy questions and data-rights requests can be sent to iracanyemichael@gmail.com.

2. Who may use Nova

Nova V1 is intended for users aged 16 or older. By creating an account, you confirm that you meet this minimum age. Nova should not be used by anyone below the stated minimum age.

3. Information we process

  • Account information, including email address and authentication records.
  • Prompts, conversations, answers and learning interactions needed to provide tutoring.
  • Saved preferences, settings, learning progress and Nova memory used for personalization.
  • Files or documents when you voluntarily use upload features.
  • Technical, diagnostic and security information needed to operate and protect Nova.

4. Why we use information

  • To create and authenticate accounts and maintain secure sessions.
  • To provide AI-assisted tutoring and personalized learning features.
  • To store conversations, memory, settings and educational progress requested by the user.
  • To prevent abuse, unauthorized access and attacks.
  • To maintain reliability, troubleshoot failures and improve service security.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. AI processing and service providers

Nova V1 uses OpenRouter to request AI model inference. Relevant prompts and other content required to generate a response may be transmitted to OpenRouter and, depending on routing, the underlying model provider. Users should avoid submitting passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information unless Nova explicitly requires it.

Nova is deployed using third-party hosting infrastructure and may use persistent PostgreSQL storage. The exact providers and applicable processing/transfer terms should be reviewed and documented by the operator before launch.

6. Retention

Nova keeps account data, conversations, memory, learning data and settings for as long as necessary to provide the account features, unless the user deletes the account or applicable law requires otherwise. Security records may be retained for a limited period when reasonably necessary to protect the service or comply with legal obligations.

Deletion requests are designed to remove account-owned application data promptly. Backups or security records may take longer to disappear where technically necessary or legally permitted.

7. Your rights

Where applicable under data-protection law, including the GDPR, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing and to request portability. Requests can be sent to iracanyemichael@gmail.com. The operator may request reasonable information needed to verify the identity of the requester.

8. Account deletion

Authenticated users can request permanent account deletion through Nova's account settings. The deletion operation is designed to remove the account, active authentication sessions, persisted conversations and user memory. Deletion does not remove shared application code, model assets or data that the operator is legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.

9. Security

Nova uses measures including password hashing, authenticated server-side sessions, secure production cookies, access controls, HTTPS, request limits, persistent storage controls and security headers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing

Nova may use infrastructure or AI providers located outside France or the European Economic Area. Before public launch, the operator should verify the relevant provider locations, transfer mechanisms and contractual safeguards required by applicable data-protection law.

11. Changes

Material changes to this notice will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Where required by applicable law, users will receive additional notice.
