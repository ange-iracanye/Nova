## 1. What Nova is

Nova is an adaptive educational assistant. This policy explains what information Nova processes, why it is processed, how long it is retained, and what choices users have.

## 2. Information Nova processes

Depending on the features a user uses, Nova may process:

- account information such as email address and authentication/session records;
- conversations and learning interactions submitted to Nova;
- learning progress, subjects, topics, mastery estimates, confidence and related educational analytics;
- saved preferences and memories explicitly created or inferred by the product;
- uploaded documents or other content when the user chooses to use those features;
- technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service, such as timestamps, request metadata and error information.

Nova should not require users to submit information that is unnecessary for the educational service. Users should not submit highly sensitive personal information unless the product explicitly supports and protects that category.

## 3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

- authenticate accounts and maintain sessions;
- answer educational questions;
- personalize explanations and learning paths;
- maintain learning progress and memory;
- protect the service against abuse and unauthorized access;
- monitor reliability, security and performance;
- comply with applicable legal obligations.
