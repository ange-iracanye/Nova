# Nova Brain

Nova's brain is divided into specialized systems.

## Planner

Decides what Nova should do first.

---

## Intent Detector

Understands what the student wants.

---

## Knowledge Search

Searches Nova's knowledge base.

---

## Memory

Retrieves useful information about the student.

---

## Tutor

Transforms information into educational explanations.

---

## Reasoner

Chooses the best strategy for answering.

---

## Language Model

Produces the final response.

---

## Reflection (Future)

After every conversation, Nova will evaluate whether the student understood the lesson and update their learning profile.
