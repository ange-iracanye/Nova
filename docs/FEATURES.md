# Nova AI Features

## Adaptive Learning

Nova changes its explanations according to the student's level, previous mistakes, and preferred way of learning.

---

## Learning Memory

Nova remembers what the student has learned, what they struggle with, and what should be reviewed later.

Nova remembers learning progress, not just conversations.

---

## Guided Study Sessions

Instead of waiting for questions, Nova creates personalized study sessions based on upcoming exams, forgotten topics, and learning goals.

---

## Progress Tracking

Nova measures mastery for every topic studied.

Instead of simply recording completed lessons, Nova estimates how well the student understands each concept.

---

## Intelligent Quiz Generation

Nova automatically creates quizzes adapted to the student's current level.

Questions become easier or harder depending on previous answers.

---

## Knowledge Retrieval

Nova searches its knowledge base before answering.

This makes answers more reliable than relying only on the language model.

---

## Local AI

Nova runs using a local language model.

Student conversations stay on the user's computer whenever possible.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Nova should become a personal tutor that knows every student's strengths, weaknesses, habits, and learning progress.
